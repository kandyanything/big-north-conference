    About Us

    Our Conference

    The Big North Conference is comprised of 41 public and private high schools located in Bergen and Passaic counties in New Jersey. Our member schools are dedicated to promoting excellence in athletics and sportsmanship.

    The conference strives to promote fair competition among member schools, provide opportunities for student-athletes to develop their skills, foster positive relationships between schools and communities, and maintain high standards of integrity in all athletic programs.

    All Big North Conference member schools belong to the New Jersey State Interscholastic Athletic Association (NJSIAA) and compete in accordance with NJSIAA rules and regulations.
